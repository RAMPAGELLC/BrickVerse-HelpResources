# How to use/activate a E-Gift Card?

{% hint style="danger" %}
**Article Notice — September 10, 2026**

On September 10, 2026, BrickVerse began rolling out a new gift card system that is operated and managed directly by BrickVerse. This replaces our previous reliance on GiftUp and allows us to handle gift card purchases and redemptions directly.

This Help Center article is **outdated and applies only to legacy GiftUp e-gift cards**. Existing GiftUp gift cards remain active and can continue to be redeemed using the instructions below.

New purchases of GiftUp-powered gift cards will be discontinued within the next week as we transition fully to the new BrickVerse gift card system.
{% endhint %}
