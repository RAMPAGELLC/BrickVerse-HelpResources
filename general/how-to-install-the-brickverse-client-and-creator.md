# How to Install the BrickVerse Client and Creator

Installing the BrickVerse Client and Creator is quick and easy. Follow these steps to get started:

1. **Go to the Download Page**\
   Open your web browser and navigate to:\
   [https://brickverse.gg/download](https://brickverse.gg/download)
2. **Download the Installer**
   * On the download page, click the **Download** button for the BrickVerse Installer.
   * Scroll down the page to review and agree to the End User License Agreement (EULA).
   * Once you agree, the download should start automatically.
3. **Install Either or Both Programs**
4. **Launch BrickVerse**
   * After installation, open the BrickVerse Client to start playing or use the Creator to create your own experiences.

**Tip:** Make sure your internet connection is stable during download to prevent interruptions.
