# AI and Platform Integrity Policy

**Last Updated:** June 2026

### Introduction

BrickVerse is committed to maintaining a safe, secure, fair, reliable, and trustworthy platform for users, creators, developers, advertisers, partners, and the broader community.

This AI and Platform Integrity Policy governs the use of artificial intelligence systems, automated technologies, bots, scripts, APIs, integrations, crawlers, scraping tools, data collection technologies, monitoring systems, commercial services, and any similar technology that interacts with BrickVerse services.

The purpose of this Policy is to protect users, preserve platform integrity, safeguard BrickVerse infrastructure, prevent abuse, and ensure that platform systems operate as intended.

This Policy applies to all BrickVerse websites, applications, APIs, services, infrastructure, moderation systems, support systems, developer tools, creator tools, advertising systems, analytics systems, and related technologies.

By accessing or using any BrickVerse service, you agree to comply with this Policy in addition to all other applicable BrickVerse agreements, policies, standards, and terms.

***

## 1. Artificial Intelligence

BrickVerse supports responsible innovation and recognizes that artificial intelligence technologies may provide legitimate benefits for development, creativity, accessibility, moderation, education, localization, workflow enhancement, and content creation.

Unless otherwise prohibited by this Policy or other BrickVerse policies, users may utilize artificial intelligence systems for lawful and authorized purposes.

Users may not use artificial intelligence systems to:

* Harass, threaten, exploit, deceive, manipulate, or target other users.
* Generate spam, scams, phishing attempts, or fraudulent content.
* Circumvent moderation systems, safety systems, age protections, parental controls, rate limits, or platform restrictions.
* Impersonate BrickVerse, its personnel, partners, creators, or users.
* Manipulate rankings, recommendations, search results, discovery systems, engagement systems, advertising systems, reputation systems, or analytics systems.
* Facilitate account abuse, credential attacks, account farming, social engineering, or enforcement evasion.
* Reverse engineer BrickVerse safety systems, recommendation systems, moderation systems, machine learning systems, anti-abuse systems, or security systems.
* Train, benchmark, evaluate, improve, fine-tune, or develop artificial intelligence systems using BrickVerse content, services, data, infrastructure, user information, moderation information, or platform materials without prior written authorization.

BrickVerse reserves the right to restrict or prohibit any AI-related activity that may negatively impact platform safety, integrity, infrastructure, intellectual property, business operations, or users.
