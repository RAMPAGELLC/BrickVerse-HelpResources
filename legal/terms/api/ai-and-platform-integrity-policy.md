# AI and Platform Integrity Policy

_Last Updated: June 2026_

BrickVerse is committed to maintaining a safe, secure, fair, and reliable platform for all users, creators, developers, advertisers, and partners. This AI and Platform Integrity Policy governs the use of artificial intelligence systems, automated tools, bots, crawlers, scripts, scraping technologies, data collection methods, and similar technologies when interacting with BrickVerse, its websites, applications, services, APIs, developer tools, and related infrastructure.

By accessing or using any BrickVerse service, you agree to comply with this policy in addition to our Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Community Standards, and any applicable developer or API agreements.

### 1. Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning

#### Permitted Uses

BrickVerse supports innovation and recognizes that artificial intelligence technologies can be valuable tools for creativity, development, moderation, accessibility, education, and content creation.

Unless otherwise prohibited by this policy or other BrickVerse policies, users may utilize artificial intelligence systems to:

* Assist with game development, scripting, design, modeling, audio creation, writing, localization, and creative workflows.
* Enhance accessibility features and user experiences.
* Create original content for use within BrickVerse.
* Develop integrations or tools that comply with all applicable platform policies.
* Analyze data that the user is legally authorized to access.

#### Prohibited Uses

Artificial intelligence may not be used to:

* Harass, intimidate, threaten, stalk, exploit, or deceive other users.
* Generate or distribute spam, unsolicited communications, or repetitive content.
* Circumvent moderation systems, safety systems, age protections, parental controls, security measures, rate limits, or platform restrictions.
* Impersonate BrickVerse, its employees, contractors, moderators, creators, partners, or other users.
* Create, distribute, or facilitate fraudulent, misleading, malicious, illegal, or harmful content.
* Generate content designed to manipulate rankings, recommendations, discovery systems, advertisements, engagement metrics, reviews, or platform analytics.
* Conduct automated abuse, account creation, account farming, credential attacks, social engineering, or phishing activities.
* Train, improve, benchmark, evaluate, fine-tune, or develop artificial intelligence models using BrickVerse content, services, systems, data, or user information without prior written authorization.
* Reverse engineer, analyze, replicate, or derive proprietary moderation, recommendation, ranking, safety, fraud detection, or machine learning systems operated by BrickVerse.

BrickVerse reserves the right to restrict, suspend, or prohibit any AI-related activity that it determines may negatively impact users, platform safety, infrastructure, intellectual property, or business operations.

### 2. Data Collection, Scraping, and Data Mining

#### General Restriction

Unless expressly authorized in writing by BrickVerse, users may not collect, aggregate, copy, harvest, extract, download, monitor, index, store, reproduce, sell, license, transfer, or otherwise process data obtained from BrickVerse through automated or manual means beyond normal intended platform usage.

This restriction applies regardless of whether data is publicly visible, authenticated, exposed through APIs, embedded in source code, accessible through developer tools, or otherwise technically obtainable.

#### Prohibited Activities

Prohibited activities include, but are not limited to:

* Web scraping.
* Data scraping.
* Screen scraping.
* Content harvesting.
* Automated indexing.
* Automated monitoring.
* Dataset generation.
* Bulk collection of user information.
* Crawling BrickVerse services without authorization.
* Building databases derived from BrickVerse content or user information.
* Extracting platform statistics, creator information, player information, social relationships, economic information, moderation information, or operational data.
* Creating competing products, services, datasets, directories, search engines, analytics platforms, or machine learning models using BrickVerse data.
* Collecting information for commercial resale, advertising, lead generation, surveillance, profiling, or competitive analysis.

#### User Data

Users may not collect, process, store, share, sell, transfer, or publish information relating to other users without an appropriate legal basis and all required permissions.

This includes, but is not limited to:

* Email addresses.
* Phone numbers.
* Account identifiers.
* Personal information.
* Device information.
* IP addresses.
* Social relationships.
* Behavioral data.
* Activity history.
* Account status information.

Unauthorized automated access to BrickVerse is prohibited.

Users may not use bots, scripts, crawlers, agents, automation frameworks, browser automation tools, emulators, robotic process automation systems, or similar technologies to interact with BrickVerse unless expressly authorized by BrickVerse.

The following activities are prohibited when performed through automated means:

* Account registration.

* Password recovery requests.
* Promotional code redemption.
* Beta key distribution.
* Content submission.
* Friend requests.

* Inventory interactions.

#### Developer Moderation and Shared Ban List Exception

Notwithstanding the restrictions contained within this Policy, BrickVerse permits creators, developers, studios, and authorized experience operators to create, maintain, distribute, and utilize moderation-related systems intended to improve safety, security, and community management within their own experiences.

Permitted systems may include:

* Private moderation databases.
* Shared moderation databases.
* Public moderation databases.
* Cross-experience ban lists.
* Reputation and trust systems.
* Anti-cheat enforcement systems.
* Exploit detection systems.
* Safety and risk assessment systems.
* Community protection systems.
* Content moderation systems.

Developers may voluntarily share moderation information with other creators for the purpose of preventing abuse, cheating, exploitation, harassment, fraud, spam, or other harmful conduct within their experiences.

Any shared moderation system must be operated responsibly and in good faith. Developers are responsible for ensuring that moderation actions are based upon reasonable evidence and legitimate platform safety concerns.

Examples of acceptable reasons for inclusion in a shared moderation system include:

* Confirmed cheating or exploitation.
* Distribution or use of malicious software.
* Repeated harassment or targeted abuse.
* Spam or malicious automation.
* Fraudulent activity.
* Circumvention of platform enforcement actions.
* Accounts used primarily for disruptive or harmful conduct.
* Serious violations of a developer's published rules.
* Safety risks to users or communities.

Developers may not knowingly use shared moderation systems to:

* Discriminate against protected groups.
* Harass, intimidate, or retaliate against users.
* Restrict access based on personal opinions, lawful criticism, or competitive activities.
* Publish private, confidential, or personally identifiable information.
* Create misleading, fraudulent, or fabricated moderation records.
* Circumvent BrickVerse platform enforcement systems.

BrickVerse reserves the right to investigate, restrict, suspend, or remove any moderation system that is used in a manner that threatens user safety, platform integrity, privacy, legal compliance, or the rights of other users.

BrickVerse does not independently verify, endorse, certify, or guarantee the accuracy of any developer-operated moderation database, reputation system, or shared ban list. Participation in such systems is voluntary and undertaken at the developer's own risk and discretion.

Nothing in this section obligates developers to honor, import, enforce, or recognize moderation actions taken by other developers, experiences, organizations, or communities.
