# Player List & Chat Badges

## Player List & Chat Badges

Badges are small icons that may appear next to a player's name in the **in-game player list** and **chat**. They provide quick information about a player's relationship to you, their role in the experience, BrickVerse account status, or participation in certain BrickVerse programs.

A player may have **more than one badge** at the same time. Some badges are permanent while others, such as the Birthday or Creator badge, only appear when applicable.

### Staff, verification, and beta badges

Some badges can look official, but they don't all mean the same thing.

The **BrickVerse Staff** badge is the badge you should look for when identifying an official BrickVerse staff member. A **Beta Tester**, **Star Creator**, **Partner**, **Verified Account**, or **Government Official** badge does **not** make someone BrickVerse staff or give them staff authority.

In particular, beta testers are regular BrickVerse users who have helped test the platform. They don't receive moderation or administrative authority simply because they have the Beta Tester badge.

### Astro membership badges

The four blue Astro badges represent a player's current **membership level**, from Level 1 through Level 4. A higher-numbered badge indicates a different Astro membership tier.

Astro badges are membership indicators only. **Having an Astro membership does not give a player staff, moderation, or administrative powers.**

### Creator badges are experience-specific

The **Creator** badge is contextual. It identifies someone associated with creating or developing the World or Universe you're currently in.

Seeing this badge in one experience doesn't necessarily mean that player created other BrickVerse experiences, and it doesn't indicate that they're a BrickVerse employee.

### Multiple badges

Players can qualify for several badges simultaneously. For example, a World creator could also be your friend, have an Astro membership, and have a verified account.

BrickVerse determines which applicable badges are displayed in the player list and chat. Badge availability and appearance may change as BrickVerse programs and account features evolve.

{% hint style="success" %}
**Safety tip:** Badges can help identify accounts, but you should never give another player your password, authentication codes, recovery codes, or other sensitive account information. BrickVerse staff will never need your password to assist you.
{% endhint %}
