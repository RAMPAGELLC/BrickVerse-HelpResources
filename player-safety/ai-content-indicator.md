# AI Content Indicator

## AI Content Indicator

The **AI Content Indicator** helps you understand whether an experience, marketplace item, or developer asset was created using artificial intelligence.

Brickverse supports creators using modern creative tools while also giving players and developers transparency about how content was made.

***

## What is the AI Content Indicator?

The AI Content Indicator is a badge displayed on eligible content across Brickverse, including:

* Marketplace items
* Games and experiences
* Creator assets (models, meshes, textures, audio, animations, scripts, and more)

The indicator lets you know if a creator has disclosed using AI during the creation process.

This indicator is informational only. It does **not** affect search rankings, recommendations, ownership rights, or moderation decisions.

***

## Indicator Types

### AI Assisted

**AI Assisted** means artificial intelligence was used as part of the creative process, but the final work involved meaningful human creativity, editing, refinement, or integration.

Examples include:

* A texture generated with AI and heavily edited by the creator
* AI-assisted concept art recreated manually
* Code generated with AI and substantially rewritten
* AI-generated audio that has been remixed or professionally edited
* Models generated with AI and significantly modified before publishing

The final asset reflects substantial human creative input.

***

### AI Generated

**AI Generated** means most or all of the published content was produced directly by an AI system with limited or no significant manual modification.

Examples include:

* Images generated entirely by AI
* AI-created 3D models published without major edits
* AI-generated music or sound effects
* AI-generated scripts or code with minimal changes
* AI-created textures used without significant modification

***

## Where You'll See the Indicator

The AI Content Indicator may appear on:

* Marketplace listings
* Game pages
* Asset pages
* Search results
* Creator inventories
* Creator profile pages
* Other locations where creator content is displayed

***

## How Is It Determined?

Creators are **required to accurately disclose whether AI was used when publishing eligible content.**

Providing false or misleading disclosures may result in:

* Content updates or corrections
* Marketplace restrictions
* Moderation actions
* Account enforcement for repeated abuse

Brickverse may use automated systems and manual review to verify disclosures where appropriate.

***

## Does AI Content Break the Rules?

#### No.

Content created using AI is allowed on Brickverse provided it complies with all applicable Community Standards, Marketplace Policies, copyright requirements, and intellectual property laws.

AI-generated content is held to the same safety and moderation standards as any other content.

***

## Why Does Brickverse Show This?

Our goal is to provide transparency while supporting creator choice.

Many creators use AI to speed up workflows, prototype ideas, generate references, or assist with development. Others create everything manually. The AI Content Indicator gives users additional context without judging or prioritizing one creative process over another.

***

## Frequently Asked Questions

#### Does the indicator affect search rankings?

No. AI disclosure does not impact search visibility, recommendations, or discovery.

#### Can I filter AI content?

Some areas of Brickverse may provide optional filtering or search preferences. Availability may vary by feature.

#### Is AI content allowed in the Marketplace?

Yes. AI-generated and AI-assisted content may be published if it follows all Marketplace Rules, Community Standards, and applicable copyright laws.

#### Can creators change their disclosure later?

Yes. Creators may update the disclosure if the content changes or was incorrectly labeled. Intentionally providing false disclosures may result in moderation.

#### Does Brickverse verify every asset?

Not individually. Brickverse relies on creator disclosures and may use automated systems, user reports, and manual reviews to identify inaccurate or misleading labels.

#### Does AI-generated mean lower quality?

No. The indicator only explains **how** content was created—not its quality. High-quality experiences and assets can be created with, without, or alongside AI tools.
