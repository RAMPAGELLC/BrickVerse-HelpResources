# Username Changes

You can change your BrickVerse username if you'd like a new identity or branding. Before changing it, it's important to understand how a username change affects your account.

### About username changes

Changing your username does **not** create a new account. Your account ID, inventory, purchases, experiences, creator content, achievements, moderation history, and account settings remain attached to your account.

When you change your username:

* Your public profile URL changes.
* Your login username immediately changes to your new username.
* You'll need to use your **new username** the next time you sign in.
* Your User ID never changes.

***

### Username change requirements

To help prevent impersonation and abuse, username changes have a few limitations.

* Your **first username change is free.**
* Additional username changes cost **500 Cubes** each.
* You can only change your username **once every 30 days**.
* Your new username must be available and comply with the BrickVerse Username Policy.

If you recently changed your username, you'll need to wait until the 30-day cooldown expires before changing it again.

***

### Username availability

A username must:

* Be unique across BrickVerse.
* Meet the BrickVerse Username Policy.
* Not violate the BrickVerse Community Standards.
* Not impersonate another person, creator, brand, or organization.

Certain usernames may be permanently unavailable because they are reserved for official platform use or legal reasons.

***

### Profile and game links

After changing your username:

* Your profile URL updates to your new username.
* Links that use your User ID continue to work.
* Existing references to your old username may no longer work once your reservation period expires.

If you've shared your profile on social media, websites, or videos, we recommend updating those links.

***

### Creator content

Your published experiences, marketplace assets, plugins, models, audio, decals, and other creator content remain yours.

Ownership is tied to your **User ID**, not your username.

Your creator name will automatically update across BrickVerse to reflect your new username.

***

### Friends, followers, and groups

Changing your username does **not** affect your:

* Friends
* Followers
* Following
* Guild memberships
* Creator teams
* Permissions
* Account progress

Other players will simply see your new username.

***

### Game development

Developers should avoid storing usernames as permanent identifiers.

Instead, use immutable IDs such as:

* User IDs
* Universe IDs
* Place IDs
* Asset IDs

Usernames are intended for display and may change over time.

***

### Developer API

Applications that cache usernames should periodically refresh them.

For permanent identification, always store and reference a user's **User ID** rather than their username.

***

### Previous usernames

After changing your username, your previous username is **reserved exclusively for your account for 90 days**.

During this reservation period:

* No other user can claim your previous username.
* You may change back to your previous username if it is still within the reservation window.

After **90 days**, the reservation expires and the username may become available for another user to register.

Because of this, we recommend updating any websites, documentation, social media profiles, or applications that reference your previous username.

***

### Frequently asked questions

#### Does changing my username change my login?

Yes. Your username is used to sign in to BrickVerse. After changing it, you'll need to use your **new username** when logging in.

#### Will I lose my games or assets?

No. All experiences, marketplace assets, inventory, and purchases remain attached to your account.

#### Will my User ID change?

No. Your User ID is permanent and never changes.

#### How much does it cost?

Your first username change is free. Every username change after that costs **500 Cubes**.

#### How often can I change my username?

You can change your username once every **30 days**.

#### Can someone immediately take my old username?

No. Your previous username is reserved exclusively for your account for **90 days**. After that period, it may become available for another user to claim.
